## 0. Outcome in one paragraph

This stage sources and ranks potential independent Gate 2/Gate 4 auditors from public professional sources, and
prepares — but does not send — a complete outreach package. It found **five** credible candidates with directly relevant
expertise: three named individuals whose published work defines the field of backtest-overfitting and multiple-testing
control (López de Prado, Harvey, Bailey), and two independent, vendor-neutral quantitative validation practices
(QuantDesigned, MEG Analytics). **No candidate is declared qualified, independent, available, or appointed.** Every
candidate satisfies the mandatory independence criteria only *conditionally*, because none has disclosed a
conflict-of-interest statement, signed an independence attestation, or confirmed availability; those are **[OPEN]** and
require direct confirmation. The strongest *fit* on the record is a named individual with published multiple-testing
expertise engaged as a scoped external specialist reviewer (§7.7), but the strongest *service* match is a boutique
independent-validation practice that already delivers audit-ready reports and states it has no downstream conflicts.
It also re-proposes the smallest defensible remediations for the three Stage 2ZO discrepancies (`D1`–`D3`) without
editing any historical record or the protocol, updates the blocker matrix, and restates the authorization boundaries.
**Nothing is contacted, sent, appointed, adopted, or authorized.** No manifest is produced, because this stage freezes
nothing (protocol §10.2 attaches manifests to versions of frozen artifacts).

## 2. Sourcing method and its limits

**[F] Method.** Candidates were sought through public web search and direct retrieval of professional profile pages,
academic pages, and consultancy service pages. **[F]** The available web-search tool returned HTTP 402 for the whole
session, and DuckDuckGo intermittently served bot challenges; evidence was therefore gathered by direct fetch of named
pages and by the search snippets that did return. **[D]** Consequently every candidate carries an explicit evidence
label, and no claim rests on a job title alone.

**[D] What was searched for:** quantitative finance and empirical asset pricing; statistical inference, time-series
dependence, and multiple-testing control; independent strategy validation and research governance; and specialist
consultancies offering independent model/strategy validation. **[F] No private repository content, no candidate contact,
and no confidential project data were disclosed in the search.**

**[O]** The tool limits mean the shortlist is **not exhaustive**; it is the set of credible, publicly documented options
verifiable this session. **[D]** Availability and pricing for all candidates are **[OPEN]**.
